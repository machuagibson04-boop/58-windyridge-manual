Wall controls

The two keypads

The keypads share the same labels but do not share the same job. The internal black keypad controls the cinema; the external white keypad is for lighting.

Internal black keypad

Inside the cinema

Cinema functions and lighting.

Try a button Press a button to see its function here and on the simulated remote screen. Demonstration only – no equipment is controlled.

Activate Cinema starts the system. Music Mode activates music mode; choose a music source afterwards. Shutdown Cinema places the cinema equipment into standby. Hold each of these for about two seconds.

External white keypad

Outside the cinema

Lighting only.

Try a button Press a button to see its lighting function here and on the simulated remote screen. Demonstration only – no equipment is controlled.

Music Mode: selects the Music Lights scene. Cleaning routine: press Cleaning Lights, then press Shutdown Cinema on this keypad to switch the lights off. Here, Shutdown Cinema switches off the lights only.

Don't mix them up: Shutdown Cinema on the external white keypad switches off the lights only. To shut down the cinema equipment, use Shutdown Cinema on the internal black keypad or the remote.

Everyday use

Operating instructions

Use the internal black keypad or the remote for cinema functions. Lighting can be controlled from either keypad or the remote's Lighting page.

01 · Startup

Start the cinema

  1. Press and hold Activate Cinema for about two seconds. Internal black keypad
  2. Or choose Activate Cinema from Quick Actions on the remote. Remote
  3. Select a source from the remote's Media page.

Activate Cinema is disabled on the external white keypad.

02 · Music

Music Mode

  1. Press and hold Music Mode for about two seconds. Internal black keypad
  2. Select the music source you want to listen to.
  3. The projector may briefly illuminate and then switch itself off. This is normal.

If the projector is still on after about two minutes, try the Play Music quick action.

03 · Shutdown

Shut down the cinema

  1. On the remote, open Quick Actions and press Shutdown Cinema. The equipment enters standby. Remote
  2. Press the red power button to complete the shutdown. Remote
  3. Or press and hold Shutdown Cinema for about two seconds. Internal black keypad

The external white keypad cannot shut down the cinema equipment.

Projector cooling

The projector may take approximately one minute to switch off while it completes its cooling process. This is normal. Do not disconnect the projector's power during shutdown.

Projector still on in Music Mode?

If it has not switched off after approximately two minutes, try the Play Music quick action on the remote. Allow the projector to finish its shutdown process.
